    About this model

    Two models are trained when the page loads: a linear regression (gradient descent) for the final score and a logistic regression for pass or fail at 60 points. Both use eight standardized features, including a sleep-gap term that penalizes sleeping far from 7.5 hours. The training data is simulated, so results show how the method works and should not be used to make decisions about real students.
